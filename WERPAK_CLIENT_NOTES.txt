WerPak Client sources, rebuilt from the jar (ThunderHack 1.7 base, Minecraft 1.21 Fabric)

- Base: https://github.com/Pan4ur/ThunderHack-Recode (GPL v3)
- Files under src/main/java/thunder/hack that come from the jar were decompiled with CFR 0.152
  and remapped from intermediary to yarn 1.21 names (script based, not compiled or tested here).
- 15 files overwrite repo files / add WerPak classes, 29 more classes were missing from the repo
  (SpotifyHud, TargetStrafe, ElytraBooster, SkyShader, TargetESP, TotemPop and others).
- Repo modules that the jar does not contain (AutoCrystal and others) are still in the project.
  If the build complains about them, delete them and their entries in ModuleManager.
- Build: Java 21, then ./gradlew build  (jar ends up in build/libs)
- Expect a few compile errors to fix by hand (decompiler output).
